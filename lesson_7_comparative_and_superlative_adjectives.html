Look and Learn 🧠

⚖️ Comparative Adjectives

Compare TWO things

Short Words (+er)

taller

faster

Long Words (more)

more beautiful

more expensive

* Always use than (e.g., taller than me)

👑 Superlative Adjectives

Compare THREE OR MORE things

Short Words (+est)

the tallest

the fastest

Long Words (most)

the most beautiful

the most expensive

* Always use the before the word!
