📖 Section 1: Reading

Directions: Read the story and answer the questions below.

Leo spent a sunny Sunday with his older sister at the Taipei Zoo because he loves animals. They took the MRT very early in the morning. They stopped to eat some dumplings for breakfast on the way to the panda enclosure. Once they arrived at the exhibit, his sister bought the tickets, then showed him where to stand. While they waited for the pandas to come out, she told Leo stories about her favorite animals.

Suddenly, a giant panda walked out from behind the bamboo trees. Leo quickly grabbed his camera as his sister pointed at the panda eating. A few moments later, Leo snapped a perfect photo of the panda holding the bamboo. His sister was so proud of his photo that she bought him a panda keychain as a souvenir!
