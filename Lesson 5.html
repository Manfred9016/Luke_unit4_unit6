Lesson 5

Singular & Plural Nouns

Let's learn how to make words plural!

1

Look and Learn 👀

The "~s" Rule + s

Most regular nouns just add 's'.

  • a pen 🖊️ ➔ pens
  • a car 🚗 ➔ cars
  • a tree 🌳 ➔ trees

The "~es" Rule + es

Ends in s, sh, ch, or x.

  • a class 🏫 ➔ classes
  • a brush 🖌️ ➔ brushes
  • a fox 🦊 ➔ foxes

The "~ves" Rule + ves

Ends in f or fe. Change f to v.

  • a thief 🦹 ➔ thieves
  • a loaf 🍞 ➔ loaves
  • a wolf 🐺 ➔ wolves

The "~ies" Rule + ies

Ends in consonant + y. Drop the y.

  • a party 🥳 ➔ parties
  • a cherry 🍒 ➔ cherries
  • a candy 🍬 ➔ candies

The "Others" Irregular

Spelling changes or no change at all.

  • a woman 👩 ➔ women
  • a tooth 🦷 ➔ teeth
  • a fish 🐟 ➔ fish
