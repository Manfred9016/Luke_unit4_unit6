Lesson 5

Singular & Plural Nouns

Let's learn how to make words plural!

1

Look and Learn 👀

The "~s" Rule + s

Most regular nouns just add 's'.

  • a pen 🖊️ ➔ pens
  • a car 🚗 ➔ cars
  • a tree 🌳 ➔ trees
  • a dog 🐶 ➔ dogs
  • an apple 🍎 ➔ apples

The "~es" Rule + es

Ends in s, sh, ch, or x.

  • a class 🏫 ➔ classes
  • a brush 🖌️ ➔ brushes
  • a fox 🦊 ➔ foxes
  • a box 📦 ➔ boxes
  • a watch ⌚ ➔ watches

The "~ves" Rule + ves

Ends in f or fe. Change f to v.

  • a thief 🦹 ➔ thieves
  • a loaf 🍞 ➔ loaves
  • a wolf 🐺 ➔ wolves
  • a leaf 🍃 ➔ leaves
  • an elf 🧝 ➔ elves

The "~ies" Rule + ies

Ends in consonant + y. Drop the y.

  • a party 🥳 ➔ parties
  • a cherry 🍒 ➔ cherries
  • a candy 🍬 ➔ candies
  • a baby 👶 ➔ babies
  • a story 📖 ➔ stories

The "Others" Irregular

Spelling changes or no change at all.

  • a woman 👩 ➔ women
  • a tooth 🦷 ➔ teeth
  • a fish 🐟 ➔ fish
  • a mouse 🐭 ➔ mice
  • a child 🧒 ➔ children
